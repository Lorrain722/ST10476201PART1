Organisation Name

Small business 
Location

Brief History
eKofini@Proper is a locally owned coffee shop that serves the Ekangala community. It specialises with freshly brewed coffee, breakfast, lunch, snack, and beverage in a much comfortable and welcoming environment. The coffee shop has become mostly likely place for meetings, families time, student, professionals and visitors that are looking for a good quality food and outstanding customer service
Mission and Vision
To provide high-quality coffee, delicious meals and outstanding customer service while creating a warm welcoming environment where people could enjoy, relax and feel at home with the memorable experiences. The vision is to become the leading and most known neighbourhood coffee shop in Ekangala by delivering exceptional customer experience, supporting the local community, and embracing digital technology to improve customer convenience
